    Why the pollsters disagree, and why it's hard to settle

    Average miss on Netanyahu's bloc in each house's final three weeks of polls, in seats. Negative means the house underrated the bloc. The bottom row is the average of all houses.

    How far each pollster leans

    Each house's estimated lean on the Netanyahu bloc, in seats, against the consensus average, with its weight in the consensus and track-record averages. Direct Polls and Channel 14 share methods, so they share one vote.

    Who votes for whom

    The turnout model splits the electorate into groups using 2022 ballot-box results, then fits that split to today's polls. A turnout change in one group moves every party in proportion to its votes there.

    How it would have done, 2015–2022

    Each election was forecast with everything learned from the other five only: pollster ratings, error sizes and the Shas correction. Ranges are the middle 80% of simulated outcomes. "Raw polls" is the plain average of published seat projections in the final week.
